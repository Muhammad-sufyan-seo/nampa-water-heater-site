What Rinnai Diagnostic Codes Point To

Rinnai's "Troubleshooting Tankless Water Heater Diagnostic Codes" document defines the six codes below. That document covers several Rinnai model families, and exact codes and steps can vary slightly by series, so we confirm the model on the rating plate first.

Code 14: An Overheat Code to Take Seriously

Rinnai describes Code 14 as an indication of overheating that should be taken seriously. The overheat sensor sits on the heat exchanger, and Rinnai's troubleshooting includes flushing the heat exchanger to rule out scale. We check for scale first, then the sensor and the conditions that tripped it, instead of resetting the unit and leaving.

Rinnai Flushing and the LC Code in Nampa

An LC code means scale has built up in the heat exchanger enough to affect performance, and Rinnai directs that the heat exchanger be flushed to prevent damage. LC is the only Rinnai code that allows the unit to keep running, which makes it easy to ignore. Nampa's water supply is considered hard, and Rinnai's manuals state that units in hard-water areas may need treated water and a more frequent flushing schedule.

How We Flush a Rinnai Heat Exchanger

  1. Shut down power and gas to the unit and close the hot and cold service valves.
  2. Connect a circulating pump and hoses to the service-valve ports.
  3. Circulate the flushing solution your unit's manual specifies; Rinnai's installation manuals describe undiluted, food-grade white vinegar circulated through the heat exchanger for at least 1 hour.
  4. Rinse the heat exchanger with fresh water and clean the inlet water filter.
  5. Restore water, gas, and power, then confirm any LC code has cleared after the power reset.

Flushing Interval for Rinnai Units

Rinnai sets the flushing interval by water hardness rather than one fixed schedule. Its manuals list an annual flush for residential units on slightly to moderately hard water. The manual for your specific model governs the exact interval, and an LC code is a signal to flush regardless of the calendar.
