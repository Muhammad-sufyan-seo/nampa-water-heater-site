Noritz Tankless Service for Nampa Homes

We service and repair Noritz tankless water heaters throughout Nampa, from Downtown and Central Nampa (83651) to South Nampa (83686) and 83687. Noritz's EZ Series is a line of high-efficiency condensing tankless water heaters built for replacing tank-type units, and like other Noritz models it reports faults as numbered error codes on the remote controller. Noritz's help documentation states which models each code applies to, and those groupings do not line up with a single product series.

Noritz work is one part of our tankless water heater repair in Nampa. This page covers what is specific to Noritz: four error codes and the models Noritz says they apply to.

Noritz Error Codes and the Models They Apply To

The four codes below come from Noritz's own help documentation. The "Applies To" column reflects what Noritz states for each code; we confirm your model on the rating plate before acting on any of them.

Error 90: Air Path, Venting, and Gas Supply

Error 90 means the unit is struggling to move air in or out, or cannot reach its set temperature. Noritz lists the air paths to check as the front cover, the internal air filter screen, an elbow on top of the unit, and the intake termination on the roof or sidewall, with the exhaust termination also kept clear. On a new installation, Noritz points more often to insufficient gas pressure, venting installed incorrectly, or dip switches not set for elevation, vent pipe size, or length. On a unit that has run for a while, Noritz points to a dirty unit or a clogged condensate drain line.

Error 29: Condensate Drainage on Condensing Units

Error 29 means the unit cannot drain its condensate, and Noritz limits it to high-efficiency condensing models. Noritz notes the condensate is acidic and generally needs to run through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain with metal piping. We trace the condensate line, trap, and neutralizer from the unit to the drain.

Error 71: Gas Valve Circuit and Power Quality

Error 71 means the circuit board cannot drive the gas valve and gas solenoids correctly. Noritz's troubleshooting measures the voltage reaching the gas valve and solenoids; voltage below the specified range points to the wiring or the circuit board. Noritz notes the code is common on newer installations powered by a battery backup or alternative power source that does not produce a pure sine wave. On an older installation, Noritz advises checking for recent electrical changes such as a new battery backup, a solar system, or another appliance on the same circuit. We measure the gas-valve voltages against Noritz's specification before replacing the circuit board.

Error 45: Internal Leak Sensor on NRCP and CB Models

Error 45 applies to Noritz NRCP and CB models, not to every Noritz unit. A water-leak sensor at the bottom of the unit, behind the circuit board, trips when enough water collects in its well. Noritz's guidance is to dry the well and sensor and look for the source; condensation can trigger it, but a code that keeps returning indicates an internal leak.

How We Diagnose a Noritz in Nampa

  1. Read the rating plate. We record the model number so each code is matched to the models Noritz says it covers.
  2. Record the code history. We note whether the code appeared on a new installation or on a unit that had been running normally, since Noritz separates causes that way for Error 90.
  3. Inspect air, venting, and condensate. We check the intake and exhaust terminations, internal air filter screen, and condensate path before opening the gas side.
  4. Measure before replacing. We test gas pressure and gas-valve voltage against Noritz's specifications rather than swapping parts.
  5. Explain the options. We lay out repair versus replacement; our repair vs. replace guide for Nampa homeowners covers how to weigh that decision.

If you smell gas anywhere near the water heater, leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside before calling us.
